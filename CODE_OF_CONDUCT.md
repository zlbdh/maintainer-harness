# Code of Conduct

This project follows the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version 2.1.

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but address harmful impact.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on evidence and tradeoffs.
- Help maintain clear boundaries around security, privacy, and contributor workload.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Submitting secrets, private data, or proprietary material to the public repository.
- Repeatedly ignoring maintainer decisions or documented safety rules.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its contributors.
