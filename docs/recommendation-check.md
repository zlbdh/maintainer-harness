# Recommendation Check

Use this page when you have inspected Maintainer Harness and need to decide what
public signal is honest.

This is not a star request. Stars are useful only when they come from people who
looked at the workflow and would recommend it to another maintainer.

## When to comment

Leave a comment instead of a star when any of these are true:

- you are not sure what the project does
- the demo failed or the report was hard to interpret
- the evidence would not help you trust an agent-assisted change
- you only skimmed the project and do not yet know whether it is useful

Use issue #5 for reviewability feedback:
https://github.com/zlbdh/maintainer-harness/issues/5#issuecomment-new

Use issue #6 for first-run feedback:
https://github.com/zlbdh/maintainer-harness/issues/6#issuecomment-new

## When to share

Share the project only when you can describe why another maintainer might want
to inspect it. The safest wording is feedback-first:

```text
I am looking at a small OSS workflow for making agent-assisted maintenance more
reviewable. If you maintain a repo, can you skim it or run the demo and say what
evidence would make you trust or reject the agent output?
```

Do not ask people to star, upvote, repost, or leave a positive comment.

## When to star

Star only after inspection and only if all of these are true:

- you understand the workflow well enough to explain it
- you would point another maintainer to the demo, evidence examples, or review kit
- the project seems useful beyond helping the author
- your star is not paid, traded, automated, requested by a second account, or
  created by an account controlled by the maintainer

If the answer is "not yet", comment with the missing evidence instead. That is
more useful than a vanity signal.

## What not to count

These do not count as outside validation:

- self-owned alternate accounts
- controlled proxy accounts
- paid stars or star trades
- bulk engagement requests
- private messages without a public URL
- copied praise from someone who did not inspect or run anything
